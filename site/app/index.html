How it works

  • MDR (merchant discount rate) is the percentage taken from each payment by the payment method.
  • Rates are set by Mixx Business Lite. Your account may have a special rate agreed with your account manager.
  • Switching a method off hides it from your checkout pages, invoices and cashier straight away.
  • A method marked Disabled by admin can only be switched back on by the Mixx team.
  • Card and PayPal payments route through licensed, PCI-DSS regulated gateways. Mixx Wallet and bank transfer settle natively.
