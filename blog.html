5 ways to get paid faster with payment links

Share one link on WhatsApp and collect by Mixx Wallet or card — no website needed.

Payment links remove the back-and-forth of “how do I pay you?”. Create a link with a fixed or open amount, share it on WhatsApp, SMS or Instagram, and your customer pays in two taps. Turn on reminders so unpaid links are chased automatically, and use a reusable link for recurring services like deliveries or lessons.

Invoicing

How to write an invoice your customers pay on time

Clear items, a due date and a pay button: the three things that matter most.

A good invoice answers three questions: what was delivered, how much is due and how to pay. List each item with a short description, set a realistic due date and always include a pay button. Invoices sent from Mixx Business Lite carry a secure payment page, so your customer can settle by Mixx Wallet, card or transfer immediately.

Retail

Running a shop counter with the Mixx cashier

Sell in store, take cash or card and keep stock in sync with your online shop.

The cashier turns any tablet or laptop into a point of sale. Add products once and they appear both at the counter and in your online store. Every sale updates stock, and your daily report shows totals by payment method so closing the till takes minutes, not hours.

Selling across Senegal, Togo and Tanzania

What changes when you sell in more than one Mixx market.

Each market has its own currency, habits and favourite channels. Keep one catalogue, set prices per currency and let customers pay with the wallet they already use. Start with the market where you already have customers and add the next once your delivery partners are in place.
