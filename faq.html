FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mixx Business Lite.

General
Is Mixx Business Lite really free?
Yes! The Free plan is completely free forever. You can create up to 10 invoices per month, run an online store with 6 products, and use payment links — all at no cost. Upgrade to Professional when you need more.
Does the platform work in my country?
Mixx Business Lite is currently available in Senegal, Togo, and Tanzania, with more countries coming soon. Contact us to check availability in your region.
Can I use Mixx Business Lite without technical knowledge?
Absolutely. The platform is designed to be used by anyone — no coding or technical skills required. Set up your account in minutes and start accepting payments the same day.
Payments
What payment methods are supported?
We support Visa, Mastercard, mobile money (MTN, Orange Money, Airtel), bank transfers, and cash on delivery depending on your country. More payment methods are added regularly.
Is my customers' payment data secure?
Yes. Mixx Business Lite is PCI DSS compliant and uses bank-grade encryption to protect all payment data. We never store full card details on our servers.
Plans & Billing
Can I switch between plans?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, your new features are activated immediately. When downgrading, changes take effect at your next billing cycle.
Support & Integration
Do you offer customer support?
Yes! We offer 24/7 customer support via chat and email. Professional and Enterprise plan users also have access to priority support and a dedicated account manager.
Can I integrate Mixx Business Lite with my website?
Yes. We offer a full REST API, ready-made plugins for popular website platforms, and comprehensive developer documentation. Our integration team is also available to help.
